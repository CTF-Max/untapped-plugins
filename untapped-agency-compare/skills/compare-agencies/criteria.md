# Comparison criteria

Use the same criteria for every agency. Priorities only change row order.

## Services (`services`)

The work the agency explicitly says it offers, with exclusions and conditions intact.

Question when not stated: Which services and deliverables are included, and which cost extra?

## Contract length (`contract`)

Published minimum commitment or renewal term.

Question when not stated: What is the minimum contract term and how does renewal work?

## Cancellation (`cancellation`)

Published notice period, exit charges and termination conditions.

Question when not stated: How can I cancel, how much notice is required, and what exit charges apply?

## Domain ownership (`domain_ownership`)

Who owns and controls the domain during the engagement and after exit.

Question when not stated: Who owns my domain and controls its registrar account, including after cancellation?

## Website ownership (`site_ownership`)

Who owns the website and what can be transferred on exit.

Question when not stated: Who owns my website and can I take its files, content and hosting access when I leave?

## Profile ownership (`profile_ownership`)

Who owns and controls the business profile, including after exit.

Question when not stated: Who is the primary owner of my business profile and will I keep full access when I leave?

## Ad account ownership (`ad_account_ownership`)

Who owns the advertising accounts and keeps their history on exit.

Question when not stated: Who owns my ad accounts, and do I retain access and campaign history when I leave?

## Reporting cadence (`reporting_cadence`)

How often the agency explicitly says it provides reports.

Question when not stated: How often will I receive a report on the work and results?

## Reporting format (`reporting_format`)

The stated report format or delivery method.

Question when not stated: Can you show a sample report and explain how it will be delivered?

## Published prices (`prices`)

Quoted prices, currency, period, setup fees and conditions; requests for a quote are not a numeric price.

Question when not stated: What are the total fees, billing period, setup charges and separate advertising costs?

## Guarantees (`guarantees`)

Exact published promises, exclusions, refund terms or statements that no guarantee is made.

Question when not stated: What, if anything, is guaranteed, under which conditions, and what remedy applies?

## Reviews (`reviews`)

Only a numeric rating out of 5 and count together, with the source URL and date read; never review prose.

Question when not stated: Where can I see your current rating and review count on the original review profile?
