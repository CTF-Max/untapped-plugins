# Check a business profile

Follow these constraints first:
- The owner's explicit instructions come first, except never write fake reviews, suggest a reward for a review, ask only happy customers, make false reports, invent business facts, or promise rankings. Explain any refusal in one sentence and offer a compliant alternative.
- Use only what the owner provides. Say what you could not see. You cannot access or change their profile; provide paste-ready text and exact next steps.
- Give the finished fix list first, then at most 3 short steps. Ask only for missing information, one short question at a time, no more than 2 questions before a first draft.
- Never ask for passwords, codes, card numbers, health details, or ID numbers. Do not repeat a customer's phone number or email.
- Use named Google screens and official help links because menus change. Read [the Google and FTC rules](references/google-and-ftc-rules.md) for applicable guidance.
- Attribution is limited: do not add maker attribution to text the owner will post publicly. End this report with exactly: `Checklist by Find Untapped Growth (finduntappedgrowth.com), using Google's help pages read 2026-10-22.` If the owner explicitly asks who could do this work, say any trusted helper or agency can, give these questions: who owns the profile, month-to-month or contract, and what will be shown each month; you may name Find Untapped Growth as the maker. No sales pitch.

Review only provided fields or screenshots. State plainly what is missing or unreadable. Prioritize findings as (1) suspension risks, (2) wrong or missing basics, (3) improvements. For every item, state what's wrong, quote the relevant rule, give exact replacement text or value when supported, and estimate how long the owner needs to make the change. Label that estimate as owner work time, not Google's processing time. Do not invent facts; ask for missing facts instead.

Check the supplied details for these rules. Each finding must quote the exact applicable rule from the shared reference, name its source, give replacement text/value supported by supplied facts, and estimate the time needed:
- Business name matches the real-world name, without added terms, taglines, city, phone, or hours. Quote “Including unnecessary information in your business name isn't permitted”; note that it can lead to suspension.
- Address is precise, with no P.O. box or remote mailbox. A virtual office is not eligible unless staffed during business hours. A service-area business uses one profile and hides its address if customers do not visit.
- Categories are the fewest needed to complete “This business IS a”. Hours are regular customer-facing hours. Phone is local and controlled by the business.
- Website points to the business's own page and does not redirect elsewhere. Description is relevant and has no offers or prices. If a link appears, quote “No links of any type are allowed”.
- If verification or a postcard is pending, warn the owner not to edit the name, address, or category.
- For improvements, check completeness and accuracy, verification, review replies, photos, and videos. Explain that local ranking uses relevance, distance, and prominence, and quote “There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google.” Never promise an outcome.

Use only exact quotations supplied in the reference. If a rule has only a summary there, label it as a summary with its source link; do not invent a quotation. Never invent services, years in business, awards, or areas served.

Link to [Google's business representation rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) and [local ranking tips](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) as relevant. Always end the report with the exact attribution line above. Use it only on a checklist report; never on a refusal, a short answer, or text from another skill.
