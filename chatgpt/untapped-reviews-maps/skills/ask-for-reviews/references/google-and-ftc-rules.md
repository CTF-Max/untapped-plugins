# Google and FTC rules this plugin follows

Read on 2026-10-22 from the pages linked below. Text in quotation marks is copied from that page; everything else
is a short summary. Google changes its pages and menus: when in doubt, send the owner to the linked page.

## 1. Google Maps user-generated content policy (fake engagement, conflicts of interest)
https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114
- "Fake engagement is not allowed and will be removed."
- Not allowed: "Reviews or ratings that have been paid for, directly or in kind" and "Content that has been posted
  due to an incentive offered by a business".
- Businesses must not "Offer incentives – such as payment, discounts, free goods and/or services".
- Businesses must not "Discourage or prohibit negative reviews, or selectively solicit positive reviews from
  customers" (asking only happy customers is called review gating).
- Not allowed: "Merchants requesting that staff solicit a certain number of reviews" and "Merchants requesting that
  staff solicit reviews that include specific content".
- Not allowed: "Content that is based on a conflict of interest", which includes "current or former employment".
  So the owner, staff, former staff and competitors should not review the business.

## 2. Guidelines for representing your business on Google
https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
- Name: "Your name should reflect your business's real-world name." "Including unnecessary information in your
  business name isn't permitted." No taglines, store codes, hours, phone numbers, URLs, or service or location
  details in the name. Extra information in the name can lead to suspension.
- Address: "Use a precise, accurate address and/or service area to describe your business location." "P.O. boxes
  or mailboxes located at remote locations aren't acceptable." A virtual office isn't eligible unless it is staffed
  during business hours. Service-area businesses "should have one profile for the central office or location with
  a designated service area."
- Categories: "Choose the fewest number of categories it takes to describe your overall core business." A category
  completes the sentence "This business IS a", not a list of services.
- Hours: "Provide your regular customer-facing hours of operation."
- Phone: "Use a local phone number instead of a central call center helpline number whenever possible." "The
  phone number must be under the direct control of the business."
- Website: it should represent the individual business location; no links that redirect to other businesses' pages.
- Description: relevant and useful, not focused on promotions, prices or sales. "No links of any type are allowed."
- "Google reserves the right to suspend access to Business Profiles" that break these guidelines.

## 3. Tips to get more reviews (asking for reviews, replying)
https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122
- "To leave reviews, you can ask customers to visit a Google link or scan a QR code." The owner makes the link or
  QR code from their Business Profile.
- "Reviews and other user contributions to Google Maps must reflect a genuine experience."
- "Offering incentives, like free or discounted goods or services, in exchange for customers to post reviews" is
  "strictly prohibited".
- "Customers must be signed into a Google Account to leave a review."
- Replying: keep it short and simple ("Customers value direct and honest responses"); personalize ("Address the
  reviewer by name and acknowledge their specific feedback"); be conversational, not promotional (no deals or
  promotions in replies); answer negative reviews constructively: protect the reviewer's privacy, no personal
  attacks, apologize when appropriate, reply in a timely way.

## 4. Manage customer reviews
https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474050
- "Helpful and positive replies to reviews can show that you're responsive to your customers."
- Owners can edit or delete their reply from "Read reviews" on the profile.
- "If you believe that a review violates Google's content policies, you can flag it."

## 5. Report inappropriate reviews
https://support.google.com/business/answer/4596773
- Only reviews that break Google's policies can be removed: "only those that violate Google policies are eligible
  for removal." "Do not report a review just because you disagree with it or dislike it."
- Report from the profile ("Read reviews" → Report → pick a reason) or from the Reviews Management Tool.
- Evaluation "typically takes several days". If no violation is found, the owner can send a one-time appeal through
  the same tool, with up to 10 reviews per appeal. Results come by email.

## 6. Tips to improve your local ranking on Google
https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091
- Local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. "Relevance is how well a Business Profile
  matches what someone is searching for." "Distance refers to how far each business is from the customer who's
  searching." "Prominence means how well-known a business is."
- Google's tips: verify the business; keep information up to date ("Businesses with complete and accurate info are
  more likely to show up in local search results"); respond to reviews; add photos and videos.
- "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google."

## 7. FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule, Q&A (16 CFR Part 465)
https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers
- In force since October 21, 2024; courts can fine knowing violations.
- Bans writing, buying or selling fake reviews, and incentives conditioned on a positive (or negative) review. The
  FTC's example: asking "Tell us how much you loved your visit" implies the review must be positive.
- Insiders (employees, family, agents) who review must clearly disclose the relationship.
- Bans review suppression through intimidation, unfounded legal threats or false accusations against reviewers.
  Paying for changes to truthful negative reviews may be unfair or deceptive.
- The FTC rule allows incentives with no condition on what the review says, but Google bans incentives for Google
  reviews entirely (sections 1 and 3). Follow Google's stricter rule: no incentives at all.

## 8. Verify your business on Google
https://support.google.com/business/answer/7107242
- Google offers one or more of: phone or text code, email, live video call ("only available within the business
  hours of your location"), video recording, or postcard ("Most codes arrive within 14 days"). The owner can only
  use the methods Google shows them.
- "verification reviews take up to 5 business days." Rare cases take longer.
- If "Get verified" appears again, "it means Google couldn't completely verify your business": try again or use
  another method Google offers.
- While a postcard is on its way: "Do not edit your business name, address, or category on your Business Profile."
  "Do not request a new code" (a new request cancels the mailed code). "Verification codes expire after 30 days."

## 9. Fix suspended or disabled profiles
https://support.google.com/business/answer/4569145
- Appeal through Google's appeals tool; Google emails the decision.
- Documents that help: "Official business registration," "A business license," "Tax certificates," or a utility
  bill. The business name and address on them must match the profile.
- Do not create a new profile for the same business.
- An appeal can be submitted once. More review is possible only if the appeal is denied and there is new evidence
  that wasn't in the first one.
- If the Google account itself is restricted, the owner must lift that restriction first, then appeal the profile.
