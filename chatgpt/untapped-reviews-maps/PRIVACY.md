# Privacy

This plugin contains instructions and reference files. It has no server, account connection, tracking, or data collection endpoint. Find Untapped Growth receives nothing you paste, upload, or say through this plugin.

ChatGPT and OpenAI's own privacy policy covers your chat content and uploads. This plugin does not change how that service handles your information. See [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/).

Do not paste customer phone numbers, email addresses, or health details. Do not share passwords, verification codes, card numbers, or identity numbers. Use a redacted screenshot or remove those details first.

Contact: max@finduntappedgrowth.com. [Find Untapped Growth privacy policy](https://finduntappedgrowth.com/privacy/).
